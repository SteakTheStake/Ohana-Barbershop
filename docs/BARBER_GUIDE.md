# Your Ohana Shop

Open **`/owner`** on your phone or tablet and sign in with your owner email and
password. You can bookmark this page or add it to your home screen.
The old `/admin` starting page now takes you here too.

There are just three tabs: **Schedule**, **Customers**, and **Shop**.
On your phone they stay at the bottom, within easy reach.
All appointment times are Albany's Pacific time, even when you travel.

## First Time

1. Tap **Shop**.
2. Turn on each day you work and choose your opening and closing times.
3. Check your services and prices. Tap a service to change its price or length.
4. Tap **Save shop settings** after changing hours or booking rules.
5. Add any appointments you already have, before opening online booking.
6. Turn on **Let customers book online** when you are ready, and save.

Turning online booking off does not cancel anything. You can still book
customers yourself while it is off.

## Book A Cut

1. On **Schedule**, tap **Book a cut**.
2. Find a customer's name or phone number and tap **Choose**.
   If they are new, tap **New customer** and enter their name and phone.
3. Choose the service, date, and an open time.
4. Add a visit note if needed, then tap **Book this cut**.

You will see a confirmation, and the cut appears in your schedule. A new
customer and their visit are saved together; a conflict won't leave a half-made
booking. You can book the next open time without the notice period required
for customers booking themselves.

## Change A Cut

Tap the visit on your schedule. Choose a different time or service, change the
customer or visit note, then tap **Save visit changes**.

- Tap **Done** when you finish. **Undo done** puts it back.
- Tap **Customer didn't arrive** inside a visit to mark a missed appointment.
- Tap **Cancel this visit** to cancel, then confirm. Its time opens again.
- Cancelled visits stay in a small section below your schedule.
- Changing only the time keeps the original quoted price.

Your personal customer notes belong in **Customers**, not the visit notes.
Visit notes are visible to the customer.

## Take A Break

1. Tap **Block time** on Schedule.
2. Give it a name, such as Lunch.
3. Choose the start time. Tap **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min**, or **1 hour**.
4. Tap **Block this time**.

To change or remove a break, tap it on the schedule.

If a cut is already booked in that time, the shop will warn you instead of
silently cancelling it. Move or cancel that cut first.

## Take Days Off

1. Tap **Block time**, then **Time off / days away**.
2. Give it a name, such as Vacation.
3. Select your first and last days away. Both days are included.
4. Tap **Block this time**.

You can block up to 31 days at once. Larger blocks may take a few seconds to
save. Keep the page open until you see confirmation. Tap the block to adjust
it or remove it later.

## See Your Bookings In Google Calendar

Tap **Shop** and find **Your Google Calendar**.

1. Tap **Copy calendar link**.
2. Open Google Calendar on the web in your browser.
3. Next to **Other calendars**, tap **+** and choose **From URL**.
4. Paste your link and tap **Add calendar**. It shows in the phone app too.

Your cuts and blocked time appear on their own calendar. Google controls
refresh timing, which can take hours or longer; use Schedule here for the
latest appointments. **Add to Google Calendar** inside a visit creates an
independent copy that will not follow later changes or cancellations. Skip
manual adds if you subscribe, to avoid duplicates. The subscription link also
works with Apple Calendar and Outlook.

The link is private — anyone who has it can see your bookings. If it is ever
shared by accident, tap **Get a new link**: the old one stops working, and you
add the new link the same way. The link needs your shop's website to be live
on the internet before Google can reach it.

## Look After Your Customers

Tap **Customers** and search by name or phone.

- **Edit details** changes their name, number, or private notes.
- **Book cut** starts a booking with that customer already selected.
- **Call** opens your phone's dialer.
- **Add customer** saves someone new without booking a cut yet.

Use private notes for their usual style, guard number, or preferences.
Customers cannot see those notes. Changing a phone number signs out the
customer's existing devices so the account stays private.

## Change Hours Or Prices

Tap **Shop**. Change your normal weekly hours and tap **Save shop settings**.
Use **Block time** for one-off closures instead of changing your whole week.

Tap a service to edit its dollar price, cut length, or extra cleanup time.
Existing visits keep their quoted price. Turning a service off stops new
bookings without deleting its history.

Changing hours does not move or cancel cuts you already booked.

## If Something Does Not Save

- If a booking says its save is uncertain, keep the page open and tap **Check
  saved visit safely**. It checks/retries the original request without duplicating
  it. If you close or reload, check Schedule before booking again.
- If a visit changed in another window, refresh Schedule and reopen the visit.
  Your older form cannot overwrite the newer appointment.
- An offline or stale warning means you are viewing an older schedule, not live
  availability. Reconnect and refresh before promising an open time.
- **Print day** prints a private snapshot for an outage. Keep it secure and
  destroy it when no longer needed. It cannot show changes made after printing.
- **Visit history** inside a visit lists changes since the reliability update.
- Google Calendar is a delayed mirror. Manual adds are independent copies and
  will not follow later changes/cancellations; avoid adding duplicates if you
  already subscribe. Make booking changes in this shop, not Google Calendar.

- Read the message in the form. A time might have just been booked by someone else.
- Keep the form open, correct the details, and try saving again.
- If your connection drops, use **Refresh** to check the schedule before retrying.
- After signing in again, check Schedule before repeating a save whose outcome
  was uncertain.
- The shop asks before discarding an unfinished form.

Use **Sign out** when you finish on a shared device.
**Advanced administration** is still available in Shop, but you do not need it
for everyday booking, breaks, customers, hours, or prices.
